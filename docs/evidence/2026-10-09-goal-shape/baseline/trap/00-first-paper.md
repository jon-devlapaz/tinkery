## Possible approaches / not accepted

### Targeted explanations after wrong answers
Benefit: Addresses understanding directly with less cost and distraction than a persistent chatbot.
Cost: May not fully address tone or varied learner questions. / candidate

### AI tutor chat beside each question
Benefit: Could provide personalized explanations and supportive dialogue.
Cost: May feel like an unwanted chatbot, increase expense, and complicate mobile use. / candidate

### Improve explanation tone and presentation
Benefit: Could reduce the feeling of being dumb without adding a new product surface.
Cost: May not explain the underlying mistake sufficiently. / candidate
