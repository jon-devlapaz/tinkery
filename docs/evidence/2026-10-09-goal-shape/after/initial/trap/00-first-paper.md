## Possible approaches / not accepted

### Improve the explanation shown after an incorrect answer
Benefit: Could directly address why the answer was wrong without adding a persistent chatbot.
Cost: May not address tone or support follow-up questions.
Undo cost: Likely low. / candidate

### Improve the tone and wording of the incorrect-answer experience
Benefit: Could reduce feelings of embarrassment or discouragement.
Cost: A warmer tone alone may not build understanding.
Undo cost: Likely low. / candidate

### Add an AI tutor chat panel next to each question
Benefit: Could provide interactive explanations and follow-up help.
Cost: May become an unwanted chatbot and increase costs.
Undo cost: Potentially high if integrated broadly. / candidate
