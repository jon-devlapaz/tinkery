## What we should investigate / proposed

Investigate how to help people revisit a choice and its context while keeping the reasons near the related work.

## Proposed outcome

A proposed way to keep each choice, its reasons, and related work connected and easy to revisit.

## Possible approaches / not accepted

### Keep decisions and reasons with each work item / candidate only

Benefit: Keeps context close to where people need it.

Cost: May duplicate information across related work.

Undo cost / provisional: Low; records can be reorganized later.

### Use linked decision and work records / candidate only

Benefit: Separates reusable decisions from work while preserving connections.

Cost: People must maintain links and may lose context if links are incomplete.

Undo cost / provisional: Low to medium; links and records can be migrated.

### Use a visual graph interface / candidate only

Benefit: May help people explore connections across decisions and work.

Cost: Adds interface work and may distract from keeping reasons clear.

Undo cost / provisional: Medium; the data may remain useful, but the interface may be discarded.
