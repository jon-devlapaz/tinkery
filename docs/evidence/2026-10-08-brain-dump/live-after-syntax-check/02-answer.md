## Possible approaches / not accepted

### Conditional candidate: keep a small record with each change / candidate

**Benefit:** The evidence stays where the next person reviews the change, if the record can stay brief.

**Cost:** The change owner must keep it accurate.

**Undo cost / provisional:** Removing the records could make past checks harder to review.

### Conditional candidate: maintain shared check history and point to it from changes / candidate

**Benefit:** Repeated checks and small edits may need less new entry, if shared history remains easy to find.

**Cost:** The next person must follow a link and may need more context.

**Undo cost / provisional:** Returning to change-by-change records could require rebuilding missing detail.
