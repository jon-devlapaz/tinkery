## Possible approaches / not accepted

### A short record attached to each PR / candidate

**Benefit:** The next person can see check results in the place where they review the change.

**Cost:** Someone or something must keep the record current.

**Undo cost / provisional:** Removing old records may make past checks harder to audit.

### A shared check history with a brief PR summary / candidate

**Benefit:** Small edits need less repeated recording, while the PR can still point to prior checks.

**Cost:** The next person must open another place to see full details.

**Undo cost / provisional:** Moving back to PR-only records may lose the wider history.
