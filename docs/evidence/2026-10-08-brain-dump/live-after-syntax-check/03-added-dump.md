## Desired experience / proposed

When someone reviews a Tinkery change, they can quickly tell what evidence exists: real-model evidence, stub mechanics, and the operator's own recognition. Small edits need little or no repeated detail. Unhelpful questions stay dismissed. Private pre-PR thinking does not become a handover.

## Possible approaches / not accepted

### Conditional candidate: keep a brief receipt in the PR thread / candidate

**Benefit:** The next person can see the evidence beside the change, if the PR thread supports concise reused links.

**Cost:** The change owner must keep the receipt accurate.

**Undo cost / provisional:** Removing receipts could make past checks harder to review.

### Conditional candidate: maintain shared check history and link to it from the PR / candidate

**Benefit:** Repeated checks and small edits may need less new entry, if the shared history stays easy to find.

**Cost:** The next person must follow a link and may need more context.

**Undo cost / provisional:** Returning to change-by-change records could require rebuilding missing detail.
