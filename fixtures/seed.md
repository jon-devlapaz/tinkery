# Make the handoff legible

Seed Me clarifies an idea before implementation.

## The question

How should a confirmed seed appear beside the downstream brief?

## Option A

Show both artifacts, linked by their handoff relationship. Keep their original paths and contents.

## Option B

Combine them into one editable document. Simpler to browse, but harder to tell which decisions belong to which artifact.

## Suggested direction

Keep both. Let the workbench explain their relationship instead of rewriting their contents.
