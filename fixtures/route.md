# Show why a skill was chosen

A routing receipt should be inspectable beside the work it informed.

## Useful context

- The task that was routed.
- The selected specialist.
- The verification result.
- The receipt's source and freshness.

## Important boundary

A routing probability is a model judgment, not permission to act.

## A useful refusal

If delivery fails, show the explicit reason. Do not quietly substitute an unverified skill.

## This example

No routing service was called. No credentials or real receipts are loaded.
